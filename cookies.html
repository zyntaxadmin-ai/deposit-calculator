This Cookie Policy explains how DepositDeadline uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can control them. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies include local storage and tracking pixels. They help websites work, measure usage and show advertising.

How we use them

Cookie names and lifetimes are set by Google and may change. The calculator's own inputs are never stored in cookies.

Your choices

  • Cookie banner. When you first visit, you can accept or reject analytics and advertising cookies. Until you accept, we do not load Google Analytics or AdSense.
  • Change your mind. Click Cookie settings (also in the footer of every page) to change your choice at any time. If you withdraw consent, the page reloads and these services stop loading; you may also delete existing cookies in your browser.
  • Browser settings. You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage may mean we ask for your choice again.
  • Personalized ads. Opt out via Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.
  • Google Analytics opt-out. Install Google's browser add-on.

Third parties

Google sets and reads cookies under its own policies. See How Google uses cookies and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
