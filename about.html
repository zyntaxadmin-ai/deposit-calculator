About DepositDeadline

DepositDeadline is a free tool that helps tenants in the United States find out when a landlord must return a security deposit, and what a late landlord may owe.

Why this site exists

When you move out, the rules for getting your deposit back depend on your state: how many days the landlord has, whether they must send an itemized list of deductions, and what happens if they are late. Those rules are scattered across state statutes written in legal language. This site puts them in one place so you can get a clear starting point in about a minute.

What you can do here

  • Pick your state and enter your move-out date and deposit amount.
  • See the legal return deadline, how many days are left or how late the landlord is, and an estimate of the penalty that may apply.
  • Generate a plain demand letter you can edit, copy, print or download.
  • Read a short checklist and answers to common questions about deposits.

How it works

The calculator uses a table of state deposit rules based on public state statutes. Each state entry records the deadline (in calendar or business days), the penalty formula, the statute citation and a short note. Entries we are less sure about are flagged "Please verify this rule", and every result shows the statute citation so you can check it yourself. All calculations run in your browser, so what you type is private. See our Privacy Policy.

Spotted a mistake?

Laws change, and we want the information to be right. If you find an outdated deadline, a wrong citation or a bug, please tell us. Including the state and a link to the current statute helps us fix it quickly.

This tool provides general information and estimates only, not legal advice. Laws change and have exceptions. Verify with your state's official statutes or a licensed attorney.
